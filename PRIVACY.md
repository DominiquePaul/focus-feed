# Privacy

Focus Feed does not collect, send or sell any data.

- Everything it stores (whether a feed is unlocked, and the daily counts behind the statistics) stays in `chrome.storage.local` on your device.
- It makes no network requests and has no analytics or tracking.
- It runs only on x.com, twitter.com and linkedin.com, where it hides page elements. It doesn't read or store what's on those pages.

Removing the extension deletes all of its data.
