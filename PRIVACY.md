# Privacy

Focus Feed does not collect, send or sell any data.

- Everything it stores (whether a feed is unlocked, and the daily counts behind the statistics) stays in `chrome.storage.local` on your device.
- It makes no network requests and has no analytics or tracking.
- It runs only on x.com, twitter.com, linkedin.com and instagram.com, where it hides page elements. It inspects page structure and link paths to decide what to hide. It does not collect or save posts, messages, account search queries or profile content. Instagram profile post paths are kept only in the current page's memory and cleared when you leave the profile or its post dialog.

Removing the extension deletes all of its data.
